## Description (EN)

```
Real-time next departures for Paris / Île-de-France public transport, right on your wrist.

• Metro, tram, RER and Transilien, with the official line colors.
• Next departure plus the following one, in minutes, with "At platform" and "Cancelled" states.
• RER / Transilien: pick an arrival stop (A to B) and the watch only shows trains that actually stop there, with their mission code. Add the return trip in one tap.
• Metro and tram: the direction of each platform is shown, so you pick the right side.
• Follow up to 8 trips. Up / Down switches between them, Select refreshes.
• Refreshes only while the app is open, and only the stop on screen, to keep your daily request quota low.
• English and French, following your watch language.

Setup: you need a free PRIM API key from Île-de-France Mobilités (prim.iledefrance-mobilites.fr). Open the app settings in the Pebble app: a short guide explains how to get the key. The key stays on your phone and is never sent to the watch.

Data: Île-de-France Mobilités PRIM. StroyCommute is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Île-de-France Mobilités, RATP or SNCF.
```
